BIR Form 1701Q — Step-by-Step Guide

Exactly what to write in every line for self-employed / 8% filers

For 8% Flat Tax Filers — Simplified

Part I — Background Info

  • • TIN: Your TIN
  • • Registered Name: Your full name
  • • Taxpayer Type: Self-Employed
  • • Return Type: Quarterly
  • • Tax Regime: Check box for 8% Flat Income Tax

Part II — Income Computation

  • • Line 1 — Gross Sales/Receipts: Total collected this quarter
  • • Line 2 — Less: Exempt Amount: ₱250,000 annual pro-rated (Q1: ₱62,500; Q2: ₱125,000; Q3: ₱187,500)
  • • Line 3 — Net Taxable Sales: Line 1 minus Line 2 (if negative, put 0)
  • • Line 4 — Tax Due: Line 3 × 8%
  • • Line 5 — Less: Tax Credits/Payments from previous quarters
  • • Line 6 — Tax Payable: Line 4 minus Line 5 → This is what you pay

Where to File & Pay

  • • File at your RDO (Revenue District Office) or via eBIRForms
  • • Pay at authorized bank, Landbank Link.Biz, or GCash
  • • Bring: Copy of return + payment proof + COR

💡 Key Reminders

  • • 8% filers do NOT deduct expenses — the rate already includes standard deduction
  • • If exceeding ₱3M annual → automatically shift to Graduated tax next quarter
  • • Keep copies of returns & receipts for at least 3 years

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I update rates, links, or deadlines?

Open your index.html → scroll to the CONFIG block at the very top → edit the values → Save → Re-upload. Everything across the site updates automatically.

Can I switch between 8% and Graduated tax?

Choose once at the start of the calendar year. Once you pick 8%, you stay on it all year — unless your gross income exceeds ₱3M, which automatically shifts you to Graduated starting that quarter.

What counts as a deductible expense?

Must be directly related to earning income — internet, laptop, phone, software, travel. Keep Official Receipts. Note: 8% filers do NOT itemize — the rate already includes a standard deduction.

How do I earn from this website?
